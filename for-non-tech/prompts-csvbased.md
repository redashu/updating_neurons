## Support Ticket Analysis Prompts

### Summarization of Top Issues
> Summarize these 200 support tickets and identify the top 5 most common issues customers face.

### Categorization Check
> Analyze these support tickets and confirm if the current ticket categories (`Password Reset`, `Order Status`, `Refund Request`, `Technical Issue`, `Account Deletion`) accurately represent customer problems.

### Trend Analysis
> From these 200 support tickets, identify any emerging trends or new issues that customers are increasingly reporting.

### Sentiment Analysis
> Analyze the sentiment of these 200 support tickets and tell me which category has the highest customer frustration.

### Response Suggestion
> Based on these support tickets, generate sample response templates for the top 3 frequent customer issues to improve support efficiency.
