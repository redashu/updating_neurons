## ✅ Step 1: Choose a Practical Product Idea (Based on a Customer Problem)

Here’s a simple but realistic customer problem to work from:

> **🧩 Customer Problem:**  
> “We receive hundreds of customer support queries every week that are repetitive and delay response times. Customers often abandon the ticket midway if they don’t get quick help.”

**🧪 Product Idea Based on That:**  
**🛠️ Product Concept:** *AI-powered Customer Support Assistant*  
A chatbot that handles common support questions instantly using AI, and escalates only complex issues to human agents.

---

## 🧭 Step 2: PM Responsibilities Mapped to This Product

| PM Responsibility        | Example (Applied to This Product Idea)                                                                 |
|-------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Vision & Strategy       | Reduce support workload by 40%, improve customer satisfaction via faster response.                     |
| Requirements Gathering  | Analyze top 50 FAQs from support tickets. Interview support agents for edge cases.                     |
| Prioritization          | Start with only Tier 1 queries (password reset, delivery status), then expand.                         |
| Cross-Team Collaboration| Work with AI engineers to design chatbot flow. Work with design for UX.                                |
| Development Oversight   | Define MVP: chatbot + escalation logic + basic analytics. Track sprint progress.                       |
| Metrics & Success       | Measure average response time, resolution rate, and CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score).                |

## 💡 Step 3: Where Generative AI Can Help the PM

| Task                   | How GenAI Helps PMs                                      | Example Prompt                                                        |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Research & Insights    | Summarize support ticket data into top issues.           | “Summarize 200 support tickets into top 5 query types.”               |
| User Persona Creation  | Generate customer profiles from behavior data.           | “Create a persona for users who abandon after 2 messages.”            |
| Drafting Specs/Emails  | Generate PRDs, feature briefs, or stakeholder updates.   | “Write a 1-pager for an AI chatbot MVP for non-tech stakeholders.”    |
| Testing Conversation Flow | Simulate chatbot interaction to identify flaws.        | “Simulate a user asking about refund policy. Highlight gaps in bot responses.” |
| Feedback Analysis      | Analyze beta test feedback and cluster issues.           | “Summarize beta test survey results into key themes and improvement areas.”    |
