## 🔹 Basics of Prompting: Structure, Tone, Intent (with Examples)

Prompting is the art of communicating clearly with a Generative AI model to get useful results. Product Managers should focus on:

| Element    | Description                                         | Example                                                                 |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Structure  | Define what you're asking and how it should be returned | "Summarize the following customer complaint in 3 bullet points."        |
| Tone       | Set the desired emotional or professional tone      | "Respond in a friendly but professional tone."                          |
| Intent     | Be clear about the goal or action to perform        | "Create a concise product update email for internal stakeholders."       |

---

### ✳️ Prompt Examples for PMs

- **Customer Insight Extraction**  
    `"Extract the top 3 pain points from these 10 customer reviews. Present them in a table with a quote for each."`

- **User Persona Generation**  
    `"Based on this feature request and usage data, generate 2 user personas with name, role, goals, and challenges."`

- **Meeting Summary Draft**  
    `"Summarize this product strategy meeting transcript into a list of key decisions and next steps."`

- **Product Announcement Draft**  
    `"Write a LinkedIn post announcing our new AI-based auto-tagging feature. Keep it engaging and under 500 characters."`

- **Idea Validation Chat Simulation**  
    `"Simulate a conversation between a user and our chatbot for this new onboarding feature. Identify if users are confused at any step."`

## 🔹 Top 5 No-Code Product Ideas for Training PMs

Use these ideas to let PMs practice prompting, build prototypes, and orchestrate agentic workflows with free tools like ChatGPT, Flowise, and Poe:

1. **🗣️ AI-powered Customer Support Assistant**  
     *Use case:* Automate common support queries from FAQs.  
     *Practice:* Prompt engineering, RAG (to pull from docs), tone control.  
     *Tool:* Flowise or LangChain + OpenAI + Notion/Google Docs.

2. **📋 Meeting-to-Task Converter**  
     *Use case:* Convert a transcript or notes into structured tasks for Jira/Trello.  
     *Practice:* Summarization, structured outputs, intent specification.  
     *Tool:* ChatGPT + Zapier + Trello.

3. **🛍️ Product Feature Naming Assistant**  
     *Use case:* Generate creative yet appropriate names for new features.  
     *Practice:* Tone control, iterative prompt refinement.  
     *Tool:* Poe, ChatGPT, Ideogram (for branding visualization).

4. **🧠 AI Co-Pilot for User Persona Design**  
     *Use case:* Build and refine user personas based on feature feedback and usage data.  
     *Practice:* Prompt templates, structured persona formats.  
     *Tool:* ChatGPT + Google Sheets + Canva.

5. **📢 AI Draft Generator for Product Communications**  
     *Use case:* Draft changelogs, release notes, or stakeholder updates.  
     *Practice:* Structure and tone control, audience targeting.  
     *Tool:* ChatGPT + Notion + Grammarly.
